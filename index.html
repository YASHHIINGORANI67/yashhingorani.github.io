Financial Time Series Risk & Trading Strategy Analysis

MercadoLibre (MELI) return research combining historical and Student-t tail-risk measures, ARMA, GARCH and MACD analysis.

ARMA GARCH MACD Student-t

How can financial time series models, downside risk measures and trading signals be analysed together?

Historical and Student-t VaR, Expected Shortfall, ARMA, GARCH and MACD analysis.

06 / MODEL EXPLORER

Research methodology explorer

Expand each method to learn how it is used in financial risk analysis.

Value at Risk (VaR)

VaR estimates a loss threshold for a specified confidence level and horizon. A one-day 99% VaR threshold is exceeded on approximately 1% of days under the assumed model.

Expected Shortfall (ES)

Expected Shortfall estimates the average loss in the tail beyond the VaR threshold, subject to the chosen estimation method.

ARMA Models

Autoregressive Moving Average models describe linear dependence in stationary time series using past observations and innovations.

GARCH Models

GARCH models describe changing conditional variance, including volatility clustering in financial return series.

Monte Carlo Simulation

Monte Carlo methods generate simulated outcomes from a specified probabilistic model to approximate distributions and risk measures.

Extreme Value Theory

EVT provides statistical methods for modelling unusually large observations and extreme distribution tails.

Logistic Regression

Logistic regression models the probability of a binary event, such as borrower default, as a function of explanatory variables.

Ordinary Least Squares

OLS estimates linear regression coefficients by minimizing the sum of squared residuals.

Maximum Drawdown

Maximum drawdown measures the largest observed peak-to-trough percentage decline in an asset or portfolio value series.

MACD Strategy Analysis

Moving Average Convergence Divergence compares exponential moving averages to generate momentum-related signals. Backtests must consider transaction costs and overfitting.
